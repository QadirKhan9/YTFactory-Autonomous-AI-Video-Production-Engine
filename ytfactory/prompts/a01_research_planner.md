Produce a research plan as JSON:
- "sub_questions": 3–5 answerable sub-questions that decompose the topic.
- "search_queries": 3–6 concrete academic search strings (authoritative vocabulary, no clickbait).
- "source_types": which source classes to prioritize (peer_reviewed, gov, institution, journalism).

Prefer OpenAlex / Europe PMC / PubMed style queries. Do not answer the topic yourself.
